## Project Overview

Cairn is facility management software designed for:

- Climbing gyms
- Recreation centers
- Camps
- Yoga studios
- Membership organizations

The goal is to provide a modern alternative to legacy recreation software.

---

## Technology

- Astro
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Postgres
- Vercel

---

## Product Principles

### Front Desk First

The system must be optimized for staff working:

- Busy check-in desks
- Membership sales
- Program registration

Fast workflows are preferred over feature complexity.

---

### Reduce Clicks

Always prefer:

- Fewer clicks
- Faster searches
- Better defaults

---

### Operational Clarity

Staff should immediately understand:

- Who is checked in
- Membership status
- Waiver status
- Household relationships
- Outstanding balances

---

## Customer Rules

Customer profiles contain:

- Legal name
- Preferred name
- Contact information
- Emergency contact
- Household relationships

Birthday visibility is important.

---

## Household Rules

Households represent relationships.

A household may contain:

- Adults
- Children

Avoid duplicate relationship systems.

---

## Staff Rules

Staff profiles should mirror customer profiles when possible.

Roles:

- Owner
- Manager
- Front Desk

Permissions are role-based.

---

## POS Rules

POS should support:

- Search
- Quick buttons
- Categories
- Sales history
- Returns

Common actions should be accessible within one or two clicks.

---

## Reporting Rules

Reports should focus on:

- Revenue
- Attendance
- Membership activity
- Program participation

Visual dashboards are preferred.

---

## Development Rules

Important:

- Minimize repository exploration.
- Avoid unrelated refactors.
- Keep workflows consistent.
- Preserve established UI patterns.
- Add tests when fixing bugs.
- Run tests before completion.

---

## Commit Rules

Create logical commits.

Do not push.

Allow user review before push.
