# Cairn Business Model

Cairn is the facility operations product built by Stone Cairn.

Production domain: `stonecairn.app`

Legal ownership: Argon Collective LLC.

Pilot and demo environments may continue to run on the current hosted deployment while Cairn prepares for production operations.

## Pricing Philosophy
Cairn is designed to support growing community organizations without penalizing usage.

Cairn does not charge for:
- staff seats
- customers
- households
- programs
- transactions
- core features

Every organization receives the complete Cairn experience. Plans are informational in the pilot product and do not restrict access to functionality.

Organizations pay based on:
- the number of facilities they operate
- the level of support they want from Stone Cairn

## Plans
| Plan | Price | Annual | Facilities | Best Fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single Facility | $149/month | $1,490/year | 1 facility | Independent facilities |
| Multi-Facility | $299/month | $2,990/year | Up to 5 facilities | Growing multi-location organizations |
| Enterprise | Let's talk | Let's talk | Unlimited facilities | Associations, networks, and complex implementations |

Public marketing should not show Enterprise pricing during the pilot. Use: "Need support for a larger organization? Let's talk."

## Support Tiers
| Support Tier | Price | Target Response | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Support | Included | Two business days | Standard product support and documentation |
| Priority Support | $100/month or $1,000/year | One business day | Priority routing and quarterly check-ins |
| Concierge Support | Custom pricing | Agreed with the organization | Dedicated support contact, staff training, workflow consulting, migration assistance, and implementation planning |

Support tiers are operational relationships. They do not gate features.

## Trial Experience
Cairn supports a 30-day trial.

Trial expectations:
- no credit card required
- full platform access
- no feature restrictions
- trial status visible in Platform Administration
- organization billing settings remain informational

Messaging for prospective facilities:

> Experience the complete Cairn platform before making a decision.

## Pilot Feedback
Pilot users can use the floating **Need Help?** button throughout Cairn.

Supported actions:
- Report a Bug
- Suggest an Improvement
- Ask a Question
- Request Training
- Contact Support

Feedback captures:
- organization
- facility
- current page
- user role
- timestamp
- freeform description

Feedback is stored locally in the current Cairn architecture and is designed to evolve into the full support infrastructure.
