# Billing

## Overview

Cairn includes a billing foundation for account balances, credits, invoices, statements, renewal activity, and billing-related alerts.

## Common Workflows
- review outstanding balances
- add or apply credit
- review invoices and statements
- inspect renewal failures

## Step-by-Step: Review a Billing Account
1. Open **Billing**.
2. Search for a customer or household account.
3. Review balance, status, credits, invoices, statements, and renewals.
4. Take follow-up action such as reminder, credit application, or temporary access review.

## Tips
- Billing is still a foundation feature; treat it as operationally useful but not yet a full external processor integration.
- Related communications and alerts are important when renewals fail.
