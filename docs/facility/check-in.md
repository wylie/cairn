# Check-In

## Overview

Check-In is designed for fast front desk use. It supports customer search, warnings, active roster review, household check-ins, check-out workflows, and occupancy visibility.

## Common Workflows
- search and check in a customer
- review why a customer is blocked
- perform a manager override
- check out a customer
- review recent check-ins and current occupancy

## Step-by-Step: Check In a Customer
1. Open **Check-In**.
2. Search by name, member ID, phone, email, or membership code.
3. Select the customer.
4. Review warnings for:
   - waiver state
   - membership/access state
   - age/household notes
5. Complete check-in, or use a manager override when allowed.

## Tips
- Customer photos matter here. They reduce mistaken identity at the front desk.
- Household review is often faster than opening multiple individual profiles.
- Closeout settings can automatically check out stale active visitors at the end of the day.
