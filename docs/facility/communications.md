# Communications

## Overview

The Communications hub centralizes outbound and internal communication history across customers, households, memberships, waitlists, programs, alerts, and staff notes.

## Common Workflows
- send an email or SMS
- schedule a message
- save a draft
- reuse a template
- review communication history on a customer or household

## Step-by-Step: Send a Communication
1. Open **Communications**.
2. Choose recipients.
3. Select a template or compose from scratch.
4. Choose send now, schedule, or save draft.
5. Review resulting history in the hub and on related customer or household records.

## Tips
- Use communication history instead of separate external notes when possible.
- Respect communication preferences and opt-out settings.
- Alerts and tasks often pair naturally with communications for operational follow-up.
