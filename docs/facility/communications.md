# Communications

## Overview
The communications hub centralizes email, SMS, system notifications, and internal staff notes. It supports customer, household, program, membership, waitlist, and alert-related communication history.

## What You Can Do
- draft, schedule, and send communications
- use templates such as waiver reminders or registration confirmations
- review recent message activity and delivery status
- view communications in customer and household timelines
- respect communication preferences and opt-out settings

## Common Workflows
### Send a message to a customer
1. Open Communications.
2. Select the recipient.
3. Choose a template or start a custom message.
4. Review the message body and scheduling options.
5. Send or save as draft.

### Message a household
1. Open a household or the communications hub.
2. Select the household recipient target.
3. Review who will receive the message.
4. Send or schedule the message.

### Review communication history
1. Open Communications or a customer/household profile.
2. Filter by email, SMS, system notification, or internal note.
3. Review delivery status and timeline context.

## Tips
- Use internal staff notes for operational context that should not be sent externally.
- Use communication preferences to avoid accidental outreach to opted-out recipients.
- Templates reduce inconsistency for renewal reminders and waiver follow-up.
