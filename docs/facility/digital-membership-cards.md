# Digital Membership Cards

## Overview

Cairn generates customer-facing digital membership cards for active access records. Cards are designed for immediate use in Cairn and for future wallet support.

## What a Card Shows
- customer photo or initials
- customer name
- membership type
- membership status
- membership number
- organization branding
- expiration date
- QR code / card token

## Common Workflows
- customer views their card in the portal
- staff reviews a card in customer, household, or membership detail
- front desk checks in by membership number or future QR workflow

## Step-by-Step: View a Card
1. Open customer portal **Membership Card** or staff-side membership detail.
2. Review status and expiration.
3. Use the card visually or by membership number for identity/access support.

## Tips
- Cards are most useful when customer photos are present.
- QR code architecture is in place for future scanning, but scanner hardware integration is not yet implemented.
- Guardians can view dependent cards when permitted by household visibility rules.
