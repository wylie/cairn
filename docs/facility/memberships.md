# Memberships

## Overview

Cairn supports memberships and access products as operational records rather than simple catalog items. Staff can manage membership lifecycle, access eligibility, renewals, freeze periods, household coverage, and membership-related reporting.

## Common Workflows
- search active or expiring memberships
- review a member's current status
- freeze or unfreeze a membership
- renew or extend access
- review household membership coverage

## Step-by-Step: Manage a Membership
1. Open **Memberships**.
2. Filter or search by customer, household, type, or status.
3. Select a membership.
4. Review status, dates, household coverage, and timeline.
5. Use lifecycle actions:
   - Renew Membership
   - Extend Membership
   - Freeze Membership
   - Unfreeze Membership
   - Cancel Membership
   - Change Membership Type

## Tips
- Membership status affects pricing, check-in eligibility, and customer portal visibility.
- Frozen memberships remain attached but should not grant access during the freeze window.
- Related alerts help surface renewal failures and expiring memberships quickly.
