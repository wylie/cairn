# Facility Onboarding

## Overview

Cairn is organized around one facility organization at a time. Each organization gets:
- a public facility landing page
- a customer portal
- a staff portal
- organization settings and branding

## First-Time Setup Checklist
1. Confirm your organization branding in **Settings → Branding**.
2. Review **Settings → Facility** for timezone, date/time format, contact information, and description.
3. Add or review **Locations**.
4. Review **Staff Roles** and **Permissions**.
5. Confirm starter **Products**, **Waivers**, and **Programs**.
6. Test core workflows:
   - customer creation
   - check-in
   - POS sale
   - waiver signing
   - membership display

## Common Workflows
- Configure a facility before launch
- Train front desk staff on day-one workflows
- Verify customer portal branding and login routes
- Review default seeded content before using live data

## Step-by-Step: Prepare a Demo Facility
1. Open the staff portal for your facility, for example `/o/summit/dashboard`.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Update facility profile and contact information.
4. Open **Branding** and confirm colors, logo, and nickname.
5. Open **Products** and check default access products.
6. Open **Waivers** and confirm active waiver templates.
7. Open **Programs** and create or edit sessions.

## Tips
- Start with the seeded demo orgs to understand the flow before changing data.
- Decide early whether your default date format is US-style or international.
- Review closeout and calendar default view settings before front desk use.
