# Reports

## Overview
Reports and analytics provide operational, financial, attendance, household, rental, communications, and executive-level visibility.

## What You Can Do
- review dashboard metrics and drill into related workspaces
- analyze attendance, revenue, memberships, programs, waivers, households, rentals, and communications
- apply date, location, and category filters
- use saved report configurations
- export report output through placeholder export actions

## Common Workflows
### View reports
1. Open Reports & Analytics.
2. Choose a report category.
3. Apply date and location filters.
4. Review charts, tables, and top-line metrics.

### Review registration or attendance health
1. Open the Programs or Attendance category.
2. Filter to the desired date range.
3. Review fill rates, conversions, waitlists, or attendance patterns.

### Review financial performance
1. Open Revenue, Financial Summary, or Memberships.
2. Compare current period metrics to prior periods.
3. Drill into underlying categories or linked workspaces.

## Tips
- Dashboard widgets are the fastest way into the relevant report or operational workspace.
- Use saved report configurations for recurring manager review.
- Always confirm location filters when comparing multi-location facilities.
