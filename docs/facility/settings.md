# Settings

## Overview
Settings control branding, facility configuration, calendar defaults, closeout behavior, operations preferences, and the information used across the public site, customer portal, and staff portal.

## What You Can Configure
- organization name and slug context
- branding colors, logo, and description
- contact information and locations
- facility hours
- date and time formatting preferences
- calendar default view
- closeout and automatic checkout behavior
- operational defaults and placeholders for subscriptions or integrations

## Common Workflows
### Configure facility settings
1. Open Settings.
2. Review branding, contact, and operational sections.
3. Save changes.
4. Verify updates in the facility landing page, customer portal, and staff portal.

### Edit the facility landing page
1. Open Settings.
2. Update branding, organization description, contact information, and locations.
3. Save changes.
4. Confirm the public facility landing page reflects the new information.

### Change date and time formatting
1. Open Settings.
2. Set the organization date format.
3. Set the organization time format.
4. Save changes.
5. Confirm dates and times update throughout the app.

## Tips
- Branding changes affect public, customer, and staff experiences.
- Check facility hours and closeout time together so occupancy numbers remain accurate.
- Keep customer-facing contact details current because they appear in receipts, cards, and public pages.
