# Staff

## Overview
Staff management covers staff records, role presets, permissions, assigned work, operational visibility, and mobile workflow expectations.

## What You Can Do
- create staff accounts
- assign role presets such as owner, manager, front desk, or instructor
- review staff profiles and avatar fallback behavior
- manage which staff can access communications, billing, reports, products, settings, and integrations
- review instructor-oriented session workflows

## Common Workflows
### Create a staff account
1. Open Staff.
2. Add the staff member.
3. Enter name, email, and status.
4. Assign a role preset.
5. Confirm the login email and workstation PIN.

### Review staff permissions
1. Open the staff profile or role configuration area.
2. Confirm what pages and actions the role can access.
3. Use the permissions matrix when you need a policy reference.

### Use instructor mode
1. Open Calendar or Registrations.
2. Review today's sessions.
3. Open the roster, attendance, and participant details.

## Tips
- Keep role presets simple. Avoid granting owner-level access to daily operational staff.
- Instructors usually need roster and attendance access, not billing or settings.
- Front desk staff benefit from strong customer, household, POS, and check-in permissions.
