# Waivers

## Overview
Waivers manage facility liability documents, versioning, expiration, public signing, guardian signing, kiosk signing, and registration/check-in enforcement.

## What You Can Do
- create and version waiver templates
- send customers into public or portal waiver signing flows
- collect guardian signatures for dependents
- review signed records and expiration dates
- enforce waiver requirements during registration and check-in

## Common Workflows
### Resolve a missing or expired waiver
1. Open the customer, household, registration, or check-in workflow showing the issue.
2. Review which waiver is required.
3. Send the customer into the waiver signing flow.
4. Confirm the new signed record exists before proceeding.

### Review signed waiver records
1. Open the Waivers section of a customer profile.
2. Confirm current status, signer, relationship, and expiration.
3. View the immutable signed record when needed.

### Use kiosk signing
1. Open the staff-authorized kiosk waiver flow.
2. Look up the customer.
3. Complete the guided signing experience.

## Enforcement Rules
- customer portal signing must only expose the authenticated customer and authorized household members
- kiosk and staff flows may support broader lookup when staff-authorized
- signed records should remain immutable even if template versions change later

## Tips
- Use alerts for recurring waiver problems or high-risk follow-up.
- Keep waiver templates versioned rather than overwriting old language.
- Use the kiosk flow for front desk recovery when public signing was missed.
