# v0.1.0 Pilot Launch Review

Cairn was reviewed as a prospective external tester would see it: public marketing site, staff portal, customer portal, platform administration, support workflow, release notes, roadmap, and documentation.

Review date: June 18, 2026

## Ready for Testing

- Marketing site, pricing, support model, Stone Cairn branding, Argon Collective attribution, favicon, and social metadata are in place.
- Staff portal covers the core operating areas: dashboard, customers, households, memberships, check-in, POS, programs, registrations, rentals, staff, reports, notifications, release notes, roadmap, and support.
- Customer portal provides understandable access to dashboard, memberships, household, registrations, waivers, billing, purchases, visits, and membership card views.
- Platform administration is distinct from facility administration and includes organization management, subscription visibility, integrations visibility, and support tooling.
- Feedback collection is available through **Send Feedback** and support staff can review, filter, and move feedback through New, In Review, Planned, and Resolved statuses.
- Release notes, roadmap, current version display, weekly release cadence documentation, and pilot readiness documentation are available.
- Demo data uses realistic organizations and date-relative activity so dashboards, reports, sessions, receipts, and alerts do not appear empty.

## Needs Feedback

- Whether recreation, YMCA, climbing gym, and program staff can quickly identify the right module from the staff navigation.
- Whether front desk workflows feel fast enough during customer search, check-in, POS, membership sale, and registration.
- Whether customer-facing portal language is clear for memberships, waivers, registrations, receipts, and household visibility.
- Whether reports provide enough early management signal for pilot operators.
- Whether the five feedback categories are enough without making the feedback form feel heavy.
- Whether planned integration, billing, and migration language sets expectations clearly enough for pilot testers.

## Known Issues

- Customer, household, and membership import tools are not available yet.
- Production payment processing is not connected; payment workflows are pilot-ready for evaluation, not live settlement.
- Apple Wallet and Google Wallet membership card support is planned for a later release.
- Some communications, attachment, scheduled-report, and integration workflows show planned capability without external provider backing.
- Demo data is representative and may still feel artificial in edge cases.
- Browser-local demo state may reset as demo seed data changes.

## Deferred to Future Releases

- Customer import, household import, membership import, validation, and guided migration.
- Production payment provider integration and reconciliation.
- Wallet pass research and mobile membership card enhancements.
- Attachment storage for feedback and communications.
- Scheduled report delivery and polished export workflows.
- Advanced reporting, deeper operational alerts, and staff productivity tools.
- Provider-backed email, SMS, calendar, identity, accounting, and CRM integrations.

## Launch Polish Completed

- Replaced visible "placeholder", "mock", and "TBD" wording on pilot-facing surfaces with clearer pilot-scope language.
- Cleaned customer login help text so it explains pilot access support instead of exposing implementation status.
- Clarified billing, POS, reports, integrations, communications, and digital membership card language.
- Updated README screenshot guidance so the documentation does not present launch materials as unfinished placeholders.

## Final Recommendation

### Ready for v0.1.0 Release

Cairn is ready for external pilot testing. The product presents a coherent marketing site, believable demo environment, organized staff portal, usable customer portal, transparent release process, and support/feedback workflow. Remaining gaps are appropriate for a v0.1.0 pilot and are documented as planned or known limitations rather than left as ambiguous unfinished UI.
