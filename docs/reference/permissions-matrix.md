# Permissions Matrix

## Roles Covered
- Owner
- Manager
- Front Desk
- Instructor / Coach
- Customer Portal user

## Allowed Actions by Role

### Owner
- all facility configuration and operational actions
- staff and role management
- integrations and advanced settings
- refunds, overrides, billing oversight

### Manager
- most operational workflows
- reporting, communications, programs, staff oversight
- typically limited from owner-only or platform-level controls

### Front Desk
- customer creation and editing
- customer lookup, check-in, checkout, POS, receipts, waiver recovery, and household support
- generally restricted from staff admin, deep settings, and integrations

### Instructor / Coach
- calendar, rosters, attendance, session notes, participant detail review, and assigned messaging
- generally restricted from POS, staff admin, billing, and settings

### Customer Portal User
- self-service only for self and managed household members
- cannot view unrelated customers, receipts, waivers, or registrations
- no staff portal access

## Restricted Actions to Watch
- refunds and discounts
- waiver overrides
- membership overrides
- billing configuration
- integrations changes
- platform administration
