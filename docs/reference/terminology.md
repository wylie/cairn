# Terminology

## Organization
A facility using Cairn. Also called a tenant.

## Facility Landing Page
The public organization front door at `/f/:orgSlug`.

## Customer Portal
The authenticated self-service area for customers and managed household members.

## Staff Portal
The organization-scoped operational area for staff.

## Household
A managed family or relationship unit containing members, roles, shared coverage, and related activity.

## Membership
A recurring or fixed-duration access entitlement, including household coverage and pass-like products.

## Access Product
A sellable item that can grant check-in or membership-related access, such as a day pass.

## Registration
A customer's enrollment in a program or session.

## Waiver
A facility liability or consent document with versioning, signing, and expiration behavior.

## Alert
An operational issue surfaced automatically or manually for staff attention.

## Task
A staff follow-up item created from alerts or operational workflows.

## Receipt
The customer-facing record of a completed transaction, including line items, taxes, discounts, and refunds.

## Digital Membership Card
A customer-facing card showing membership identity, status, and a check-in token.
